# Denylist of terms that must never appear in consumer-facing copy or asset filenames
# Source: File 01 §5.2 and File 06 §8

# Prohibited trade bodies & marks
AA Approved
RAC Approved
TrustMyGarage
Good Garage Scheme
BOSCH Car Service

# Prohibited review platforms
Trustpilot
Google Reviews
Yelp
TripAdvisor

# Prohibited superlative claims
cheapest
lowest price
guaranteed savings
100% safe
best mechanic
# Note: make names are allowed in plain-text compatibility phrases ('we service most makes')
# but not with approval claims
approved dealer
official partner
authorised repairer
