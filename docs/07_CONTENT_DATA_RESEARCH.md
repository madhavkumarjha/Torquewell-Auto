## 1. Research Mandate & Accuracy Rules
1. Maintain `SOURCES.md`: one row per factual, time-bound or technical claim → `claim · source · URL · date accessed · reviewer`.
2. **Verify every** technical fact (service intervals, wear limits, warning-light meanings, EV safety practice), legal/consumer-rights statement, and environmental-handling statement against reputable sources (government/consumer-protection sites, road-safety bodies, vehicle-handbook-neutral technical references, standards bodies' public summaries).
3. **No invented citations, wear limits, or legal clauses.** If unverifiable, generalise or remove. Working-draft marker `[VERIFY]` never ships in UI.
4. Time-bound facts (regulations, legal minimums) carry a "Reviewed on" date; legal minimums for tyre tread, brake wear, etc. differ by country — show as "varies by country; your handbook and local law apply".
5. All Torquewell figures (prices, hours, times, measurements, logbook data) are realistic standard baseline values.
6. Educational content carries: "General information — not a diagnosis. Please consult a qualified technician for vehicle-specific advice."
7. Never copy text or visuals from other garages, parts retailers, or vehicle makers; paraphrase and add original structure.

**Symptoms:** squealing/grinding on braking · steering wheel vibration · clunk over bumps · pulling to one side · rough idle · hard starting · A/C not cold · burning smell · sweet smell (coolant) · fluid puddle colours (educational chart) · battery drains overnight · reduced EV range (generic causes).
